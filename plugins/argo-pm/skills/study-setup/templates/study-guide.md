## Data collection
- Each RA who will complete the proforma / perform patient interviews must first complete
  **3 patients under direct observation of the Site PI**, followed by debriefing. Once those 3
  patients' data are confirmed acceptable by the PI and Research Manager, the RA may recruit
  additional patients.
- The **eligibility form** is filled out to determine if the patient can be enrolled.
- The **consent form** is administered.
- Data are collected on a **paper questionnaire**, then entered into REDCap. Collect from the
  patient, case notes, and presenting physician as specified per section below:
  - [SECTION → SOURCE mapping, e.g. "Demographics, History of Present Illness — collect from
    patient at first visit"]
  - [e.g. "Medical History — patient, medical record, and physician at first visit"]
  - [e.g. "Imaging, Biopsy pathology — medical record and physician"]
  - [e.g. "Treatment and Follow-up — patient, medical record, physician; review every 3 months"]
- Initial data collection is moved to REDCap; further clinical data are entered as the patient
  undergoes treatment, as soon as updated on the paper questionnaire (scanned and uploaded).
- **Follow-up:** [FOLLOW_UP_INTERVAL, e.g. every three months].
- **Weekly accrual report:** sent to [ACCRUAL_CONTACT, EMAIL] by [DAY/TIME, e.g. 5 PM Tuesday]
  with: Number recruited; Number uploaded into REDCap.

## Eligibility form administration
- **Form to use:** [ELIGIBILITY_CHECKLIST_NAME]
- Note the consent location depending on where the patient is consented.
- For eligibility issues, contact [ELIGIBILITY_CONTACT(S)].
- Eligibility should be checked and confirmed as soon as the patient is approached.
- Leave the **"REDCap number"** section blank until the participant is being entered into REDCap.
- Once the participant has consented, the consenting professional/physician or research staff
  should print full name, sign, and date the eligibility checklist. Ensure data is accurate and
  complete before signing. Follow the ARGO SOP for Eligibility procedures.
- **Non-consent:** if the patient declines, record the reason in the appropriate box and store
  the checklist separately from consenting patients. Do not administer the questionnaire.
